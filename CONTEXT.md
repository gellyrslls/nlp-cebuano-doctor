# Cebuano Doctor

A conversational healthcare consultation and triage system that bridges Cebuano-speaking patients with clinical AI reasoning through a 3-stage circular translation and inference pipeline.

## Language

### Healthcare & Clinical Concepts

**Chief Complaint**:
The primary symptom or health concern expressed by the patient in their own words.
_Avoid_: Query, prompt, input text

**Medical Guidance**:
The clinical explanation, preliminary triage advice, and non-prescriptive home-care recommendations generated by the medical reasoning model.
_Avoid_: Prescription, formal diagnosis, answer

**Stage Evaluation**:
An isolated assessment of linguistic accuracy or clinical soundness at a single boundary within the circular pipeline.
_Avoid_: Benchmark, unit test

**Linguistic Nuance**:
A cultural idiom, vernacular symptom phrase, or code-switched expression whose intended clinical meaning requires contextual translation rather than literal word substitution.
_Avoid_: Slang, grammatical error

### Cebuano Medical Idioms

**Panuhot**:
A cultural condition attributing bodily aches, muscle tension, bloating, or stiffness to exposure to cold drafts, sudden temperature shifts, or sweat drying on the body.
_Avoid_: Common cold, influenza

**Pasmo**:
A condition characterized by trembling, dizziness, headache, or stomach discomfort triggered by delayed meals or working on an empty stomach.
_Avoid_: Starvation, hunger pangs

**Pamaol**:
Muscular soreness and body stiffness resulting from unaccustomed or strenuous physical exertion.
_Avoid_: Arthritis, general weakness

**Bughat**:
A perceived bodily relapse or severe fatigue resulting from premature physical exertion before full recovery from illness or childbirth.
_Avoid_: Clinical relapse, reinfection

**Kalibanga**:
Frequent passage of loose or watery stools (diarrhea).
_Avoid_: Indigestion, general stomachache
