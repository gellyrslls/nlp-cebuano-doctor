01. Clinical Context and Linguistic Challenges

Problem Statement

Linguistic Reality in Central Visayas

  • Cebuano speakers express physical distress using culture-bound somatic idioms (such as panuhot, pasmo, kabuhi, pamalaybalay, pamaol, lupot).
  • Patients in community consultations describe symptoms in vernacular idioms rather than standard clinical terminology.
  • Frontier clinical LLMs (such as Google DeepMind's MedGemma) are trained almost exclusively on English biomedical literature and international clinical guidelines.

Failure Mode of Direct LLM Prompting

  • Direct colloquial Cebuano prompts fed to English clinical LLMs cause catastrophic semantic distortion, hallucinated triage, or missed emergency red flags.
  • Single-pass black-box responses conceal whether reasoning failures originated in language understanding (NLU) or clinical inference.
  • Architectural Solution: A transparent, 3-stage circular translation and reasoning architecture running 100% offline via local Ollama models.
Academic AI Prototype: Developed for CS 5101 NLP. Provides preliminary triage education only and is NOT a substitute for professional physician evaluation. In a medical emergency, immediately seek hospital care.

03. Local Execution, Privacy, and Hardware Architecture

Infrastructure

100% Air-Gapped Privacy

Sensitive medical inquiries remain strictly on localhost (http://localhost:11434). Operating system Wi-Fi can be completely disabled. Zero patient health data leaks to cloud APIs.

Zero Token Exhaustion

Dr. Mariano cautioned against token maxing and recurring API billing. Local offline models provide unlimited inferences, enabling exhaustive batch evaluation and clinical testing at zero marginal cost.

Secondary Drive Architecture

Ollama model directory permanently configured on SSD Partition D (D:\files\ollama\models), safeguarding C: drive partition (~4.6 GB free) from model storage exhaustion.

Dual Execution Modes

Live Ollama Daemon: Real local LLM multi-stage clinical execution (~3.5 min end-to-end turnaround on local laptop CPU/GPU).
Offline Mock Mode: Deterministic, sub-50ms test execution for instant UI demonstrations, test suites, and CI pipelines.

06. Evaluation Methodology: 4-Axis Quality Rubric

Evaluation Suite

As required by Dr. Mariano, the system was evaluated across 5 diverse patient scenarios along 4 critical dimensions:

Axis 1: Translation Fidelity (NLU)

Does Gemma 4 capture the colloquial symptom description without loss of critical clinical meaning? Verified through semantic alignment between Cebuano idioms and clinical English representation.

Axis 2: Medical Reasoning Soundness (Inference)

Is MedGemma's clinical advice safe, accurate, and free of medical hallucinations? Enforces non-prescriptive supportive care, antimicrobial stewardship, and emergency red flags.

Axis 3: Cultural Naturalness (NLG)

Does Gemma 4 translate English clinical guidance into warm, understandable Cebuano that an ordinary patient and their family can comfortably understand without confusing jargon?

Axis 4: System Latency and Performance

Quantitative millisecond response times measured per stage and end-to-end to assess edge deployment viability on consumer hardware.

09. Medical Safety and Antimicrobial Stewardship

Clinical Ethics

Antimicrobial Stewardship

When patients inquire about self-administering antibiotics (such as ciprofloxacin for diarrhea or amoxicillin for cough), the pipeline strictly prohibits unindicated use, citing resistance and gut flora toxicity.

No Prescription Rule

The system is strictly prompt-engineered against prescribing regulated pharmaceuticals or calculating drug dosages. Advice is limited to evidence-backed supportive care (ORS, hydration, rest, 20-20-20 rule).

Red Flag Surveillance

Every response actively screens for alarming symptoms: dyspnea, cyanosis, rust sputum, hematochezia, syncope, or thunderclap headaches, mandating immediate emergency triage.

Universal Clinical Disclaimer: All consultations conclude with a clear, culturally respectful reminder that AI is an educational triage tool and urging an in-person visit to a licensed physician or Barangay Health Worker.

12. Key Learnings and Course Reflections

Reflections

Natural Language Processing Takeaways

  • NLU is harder than NLG in Philippine languages: Parsing colloquial folk medicine metaphors demands precise contextual prompt engineering.
  • Circular pipelines enable interpretability: Without intermediate English representations, auditing AI clinical reasoning is impossible.
  • Model Distillation is democratizing AI: Google DeepMind's distilled Gemma 4 weights bring frontier multi-lingual capabilities to personal laptops.

Engineering Insights

  • Seam isolation pays dividends: Decoupling the model provider via protocols made unit testing instant without multi-gigabyte downloads.
  • Antimicrobial stewardship is essential: Community chatbots must actively discourage antibiotic misuse rather than remaining passive.
  • Graceful error recovery is mandatory: Handling daemon disconnects gracefully ensures high clinical reliability.
