[11:23 – 19:26] Model 1: Gemma 4 by Google DeepMind
Dr. Vladimir Mariano:
"Next one is Gemma 4. Gemma 4 was released by Google DeepMind."
(Dr. Mariano navigates to the Google DeepMind page for Gemma 4).
"This was released April 2026, just a few months ago. There have been very good reviews of this, and I tried it myself for computer vision—I uploaded a picture and asked Gemma 4 to describe it, and it did quite well.
It is also very fluent in many languages. It is created using a process called distilling. Distilling is when they have a very, very large model—maybe hundreds of billions or more than a trillion parameters—and they create a smaller brain/model by distilling knowledge from the bigger one. Many prompts are run on the big model, and the responses are used to train the smaller model, such as Gemma 4.
You can use it for agentic workflows, coding assistants, or analyzing documents. It's not as good as models running in the cloud like Gemini, GPT, or Claude, but these local models are getting better and better. Soon, these local models will be enough for most of our needs—summarizing long documents, simple coding, or translation.
What is most interesting for me is that it is fluent in 140 languages, including Philippine languages like Tagalog, Ilocano, Cebuano, and a little bit of Hiligaynon."

📝 Lecture Summary
1. Overview & Context
* Class: CS 5101 – Natural Language Processing (NLP), University of San Carlos.
* Instructor: Dr. Vladimir Mariano.
* Core Topic: Introduction and technical walkthrough for Assignment 1: The Cebuano Doctor.

* Work Mode: Can be done individually or in pairs.
________________


2. Assignment Objective & Pipeline
The goal is to build a local, privacy-preserving conversational agent ("The Cebuano Doctor") that handles Cebuano healthcare queries through a multi-model translation and medical inference chain:
1. User Query: User asks a medical question/expresses symptoms in Cebuano.
2. Translation to English: Gemma 4 translates the Cebuano query into English.
3. Medical Reasoning: MedGemma takes the English query and generates an expert medical response in English.
4. Translation to Cebuano: Gemma 4 translates MedGemma’s English medical advice back into Cebuano.
5. Output: The Cebuano response is displayed to the user.
________________


3. Required Software & Models (Local Deployment)
* Ollama (ollama.com): Open-source tool to run LLMs locally on personal hardware.
   * Advantages discussed: Data privacy (no sensitive medical queries sent to the cloud), offline operation, and zero API token costs.
   * Hardware considerations: Allocating virtual memory/swap space on an SSD if local RAM is constrained.
* * Gemma 4 (Google DeepMind):
   * Released April 2026; trained via model distillation from larger frontier models (Gemini 3).
   * Highly multilingual across 140 languages, specifically supporting Philippine languages (Tagalog, Cebuano, Ilocano, Hiligaynon).
* * MedGemma (Google DeepMind):
   * Specialized open-weights medical model fine-tuned on healthcare literature, symptoms, diagnosis, and triage guidelines.
* ________________


4. Evaluation Requirements
Students must test the chatbot using at least 5 real-world prompts collected from other people (preferably friends/family in healthcare or nursing) covering both simple ailments and complex symptoms. The evaluation must answer:
* Translation Accuracy (Cebuano 
* →
* →
*  English): Did Gemma 4 capture colloquial symptom descriptions without losing meaning?
* Medical Quality: Was MedGemma's advice accurate, sound, and free of medical hallucinations?
* Translation Accuracy (English 
* →
* →
*  Cebuano): Is the translated medical guidance clear and culturally natural?
* System Metrics: Qualitative and quantitative performance (e.g., latency, response time based on local hardware specs).
